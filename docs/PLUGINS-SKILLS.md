# Plugins and skills

- **Skill Maker / plugin generator**: describe what you want; Vortex writes the plugin or skill.
- **Icon**: upload a 256 × 256 PNG (other sizes are cropped and resized), click **AI icon**, or keep the Red-XAI logo.
- **Upload to the store** requires your RedX sign-in. Store items list with name, icon and author.
- Installed plugins and skills are available in chat and to agents. MCP servers: see [CHAT-FEATURES.md](CHAT-FEATURES.md).
